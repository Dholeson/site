Discover Cleaner

A companion for Spotify and Last.fm. It clears songs from your "to listen" playlist once you've actually heard them, and turns your listening history into reports and artist pages.

Open the live demo → Source on GitHub

Runs in your browser: no account, nothing to install. Spotify is simulated.

Open the live demo →

The demo is the real interface running in your browser, on my own listening history (Last.fm scrobbles plus the app's own plays) frozen on Oct 4, 2026. Spotify is simulated: let a backlog song play for 15 seconds, watch it clear, then undo it. Lyrics and Spotify's discographies aren't included, and the demo says so where they'd appear. Album covers load from Spotify's image server; everything else comes from this site.

Three jobs

  1. Clears your backlog as you listen

    Point it at a playlist, say the one your Discover Weekly finds pile up in. Once a song from it has really played for a few seconds, it's removed, so the backlog shrinks by itself. You always see what will happen to the song playing and when, and you can keep it, clear it now, like it or undo a clear.

  2. Shows your listening like Last.fm Pro

    Reports for any week, month, year or all time, built from your whole Last.fm history plus what the app hears: top artists, albums and tracks with change against the period before, how much was new, your listening clock, streaks, and a year-in-music page.

  3. Gives every artist a page

    Your plays, rank, first and last listen, plays per year, top tracks and albums, what you haven't heard yet, how much of their discography you've explored, and similar artists with your own play counts next to them.

Run it yourself

Offline, in about a minute

The whole app on your computer with a simulated Spotify, so you need neither a Spotify account nor Premium. You need Python 3.10 or newer. 

git clone https://github.com/Dholeson/spotify-discover-cleaner.git
cd spotify-discover-cleaner
run.bat --demo     # Windows
./run.sh --demo    # macOS / Linux

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8888.

With your own Spotify

  • Spotify Premium on the account that creates the developer app. That's Spotify's rule, not mine.
  • Your own Spotify developer app. It's free and takes about five minutes; the app's Setup screen walks you through it.
  • Up to 5 users per app. A developer app in Development Mode is limited to 5 users, so friends either get added to yours or make their own.
  • Last.fm is optional but recommended: your whole scrobble history feeds the reports.

Everything stays on your computer. The app talks to Spotify, Last.fm and LRCLIB (for lyrics) directly; there's no server of mine in between.

How it works

One ledger for every listen

Reports and artist pages all read from one table with a row per listen. It merges Last.fm scrobbles with the plays the app sees itself, and a listen that shows up in both is counted once. Mine holds 49,548 plays going back to August 2021, and its April 2026 total is within 2 plays of Last.fm's own report for that month (661 vs 659).

Clearing that fails safe

A song is removed only after it has really played past the threshold (15–30 seconds by default): pausing stops the count and skipping ahead adds nothing. Nothing is removed until the app has read the whole playlist, a removal Spotify refuses is queued and retried, and every clear can be undone for 30 minutes, back in its original position.

Why there's no "Log in with Spotify"

Spotify's developer rules changed in February 2026: an app in Development Mode is limited to 5 users, its owner needs Premium, and wider access is only for registered businesses with 250,000 monthly users. A hosted version anyone could sign in to isn't possible, so this page runs a simulated demo and the real thing is something you run yourself.
