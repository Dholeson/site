Products I've shipped

I'm a Senior Product Manager. These are products I've shipped on my own time by directing AI coding agents: I own the problem, the decisions and the testing, and the agents write most of the code. Each one has a short case study, and a link to the real, working thing wherever there is one.

  1. Frame itPin down who it's for, what done looks like, and what it must never get wrong.
  2. Split itBreak the work into pieces agents can build in parallel without colliding.
  3. Verify itTests, side-by-side measurement and hands-on use. Nothing ships on trust.
  4. Ship itDecide what's good enough, deploy it, and keep it running for real users.
