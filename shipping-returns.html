Shipping, returns and testing

What it costs to ship, when it goes out, what happens if a console arrives dead, and what "tested" means here.

Shipping

What's in the orderShipping
Accessories only (controllers, cables, memory cards, kits)$6.00
Anything with a console in it$18.00
  • One flat rate per order, set by the biggest box. Accessories ordered with a console ride in the console's box, so they add nothing to shipping.
  • Orders are packed and shipped within two business days, from the US. You get a tracking number by email when yours goes out.
  • US addresses only for now.

Returns

  • Consoles: 30-day return window if it does not power on.

How units are tested

  • Every console is opened, cleaned, and put through a full boot before it's listed.
  • Controller sticks are measured with the drift tester on this site, so "no drift" is a reading, not an opinion.
  • Refurbished units marked "one of one" are the exact unit in the photos, not a stock image.

Paying

  • Checkout is Stripe's. Card details go to Stripe and never reach this site; Stripe emails your receipt.
  • When you check out with a one-of-one unit, it's held for you for about half an hour while you pay, so nobody else can buy it out from under you.

Your data

  • To ship your order, the store keeps the name, email and shipping address you give Stripe, and what you bought.
  • Your cart lives in your own browser (local storage) until you check out.
  • Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies.
