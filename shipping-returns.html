Shipping

What's in the orderShipping
Accessories only (controllers, cables, memory cards)$6.00
Anything with a console in it$18.00

One flat rate per order, set by the biggest box. Accessories ordered with a console ride in its box, so they add nothing.

Orders are packed and shipped within two business days, from the US. You get a tracking number by email when yours goes out.

US addresses only for now.

Returns

Consoles: If a console doesn't power on, there's a 30-day return window.

Something wrong with an order? Email support@dhole.dev with your order number and what happened.

If a unit sells to two people at the same moment, the second buyer gets a full refund.

Testing

Every console is opened, cleaned and put through a full boot before it's listed.

Each listing's photos are of that exact unit, not a stock image.

Where a listing gives a stick reading, it was taken with the drift tester on this site, and the listing says how.

Paying

You pay by card on Stripe's checkout page. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach this site, and Stripe emails your receipt.

When you check out, each unit in your order is held for you for about 30 minutes while you pay, so nobody else can buy it in the meantime. If the payment isn't finished by then, the unit goes back on sale.

Your data

To ship your order, the store keeps the name, email address and shipping address you give Stripe, and what you bought.

Your cart is kept in your own browser's local storage until you check out, along with the link to any checkout you've started, which lets you go back to it.

Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies.

Contact

Questions before or after you buy? Email support@dhole.dev.
